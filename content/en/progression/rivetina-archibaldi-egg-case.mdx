## Practical Tips for Keepers and Citizen Scientists

Captive rearing is where most ootheca knowledge gets generated, and it is also where most bad information starts. Community reports from mantis keepers consistently show that ventilation, stable humidity, and patience matter more than any temperature "recipe." A sealed container with no airflow will grow mold on an ootheca faster than almost anything else.

- **Ventilate well.** Mesh lids beat drilled holes; stale, humid air invites fungus.
- **Mist indirectly.** Light misting on the container wall, not directly on the case.
- **Keep the ootheca upright as found.** Preserve the original orientation.
- **Avoid heat spikes.** Warmth speeds development but can kill eggs outright.
- **Separate nymphs early.** Sibling cannibalism is normal, not a failure.
- **Document everything.** Date, humidity, temperature, emergence count — that is how a blank page gets filled.

For anyone handling the Rivetina archibaldi egg case specifically, the honest position is that you would be generating first-of-their-kind observations. Treat that responsibility accordingly: photograph, measure, record, and share the data rather than the specimen.
