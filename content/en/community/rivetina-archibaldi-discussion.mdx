### A genus that mostly means "dry ground"

*Rivetina* belongs to Rivetinidae, a family of ground-associated mantises that favor open, arid, and semi-arid landscapes stretching from North Africa through the Middle East into Central Asia. They are runners and ambushers rather than canopy climbers, and many species are frustratingly similar to one another in the field.

## How to Join the Rivetina archibaldi Discussion Without Spreading Bad IDs

Anyone can contribute usefully. The trick is to add evidence instead of noise.

- **Cite the description, not a screenshot.** Quote section names so others can verify quickly.
- **Photograph the specimen properly.** Dorsal habitus, lateral profile, and a clear view of the abdomen and cerci.
- **Note the sex.** The published key is male-based, so a female photo cannot be resolved the same way.
- **Record locality responsibly.** Country and province level is usually enough for public posts; precise coordinates for a narrow-range species can invite poaching pressure.
- **Say "I don't know" out loud.** Uncertain threads age better than confident ones that turn out wrong.
- **Ask before you buy.** If someone sells "Rivetina archibaldi," request provenance and photos of the actual animal.
